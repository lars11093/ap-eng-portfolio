Open with the situation, not with a summary of the task. What were you asked to
do, and what was actually difficult about it?

## What I did

The work itself. Concrete details beat general statements: what you wrote, said,
built or read.

## What I got wrong, and what I would change

This is the part that carries the marks. Reflection means naming something that
did not work and saying what you would do differently, not writing that it was
interesting.

## What I am taking forward

One or two specific things, small enough that you could actually check them later.
